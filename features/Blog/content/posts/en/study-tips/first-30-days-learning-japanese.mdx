Starting Japanese can feel overwhelming. What do you learn first? How fast should you go? What if you're doing it wrong?

This 30-day roadmap eliminates the guesswork. Follow this day-by-day plan, and by Day 30, you'll read hiragana fluently, recognize katakana, know 100+ words, understand basic grammar, and introduce yourself in Japanese!

Let's begin your journey. 🚀

<InfoBox type='success'>
  This roadmap is designed for 30-45 minutes of daily study. Consistent short
  sessions beat irregular long sessions – every single time!
</InfoBox>

## Week 1: Hiragana Foundation 🌱

### Day 1: Begin Your Journey ✨

**Today's goals:**

- Understand WHY you're learning (motivation matters!)
- Learn about Japanese writing systems
- Learn the first 5 hiragana: あ い う え お

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Write down YOUR reason for learning Japanese (5 min)
2. Read about hiragana vs katakana vs kanji (10 min)
3. Learn vowels あ い う え お with [PThamSS](/en/kana/train) (20 min)
4. Practice writing each character 5 times (5 min)

**By end of day:** You can read and write the 5 vowels!

---

### Day 2: K-Line Hiragana

**Today's goals:**

- Learn か き く け こ
- Review yesterday's vowels
- Understand how consonant + vowel creates characters

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Review あいうえお (5 min)
2. Learn か き く け こ with practice (20 min)
3. Mix practice: all 10 characters (10 min)
4. Write new characters 5 times each (5 min)

**First words:** Try reading: かき (kaki - persimmon), あか (aka - red)!

---

### Day 3: S-Line Hiragana

**Today's goals:**

- Learn さ し す せ そ
- Learn your first greetings

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Quick review of all previous characters (5 min)
2. Learn さ し す せ そ (20 min)
3. Learn first phrases:
   - おはよう (ohayou - good morning)
   - こんにちは (konnichiwa - hello) - preview!
4. Mixed practice (10 min)

---

### Day 4: T-Line Hiragana

**Today's goals:**

- Learn た ち つ て と
- Note the irregular sound: つ (tsu)

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Review (5 min)
2. Learn た ち つ て と (20 min)
3. Practice recognizing in words:
   - つき (tsuki - moon)
   - たこ (tako - octopus)
4. Writing practice (10 min)

---

### Day 5: N-Line Hiragana

**Today's goals:**

- Learn な に ぬ ね の
- Learn useful words with these characters

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Review all previous (10 min)
2. Learn な に ぬ ね の (15 min)
3. New vocabulary:
   - ねこ (neko - cat) 🐱
   - いぬ (inu - dog) 🐕
   - なに (nani - what)
4. Practice (10 min)

---

### Day 6: H-Line Hiragana

**Today's goals:**

- Learn は ひ ふ へ ほ
- Understand は as topic marker (pronounced "wa")

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Review (5 min)
2. Learn は ひ ふ へ ほ (15 min)
3. Learn your first sentence:
   - これは＿＿です (This is \_\_\_\_)
4. Introduction vocabulary (10 min)

---

### Day 7: Week 1 Review & Rest 🎯

**Today's goals:**

- Review all 30 characters learned
- Consolidate with reading practice
- Celebrate progress!

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Full character review quiz (15 min)
2. Try reading simple words (15 min)
3. Light review of vocabulary learned (10 min)

**Week 1 checkpoint:**

- ✅ 30 hiragana characters (あ-ほ lines)
- ✅ 10+ vocabulary words
- ✅ Basic greeting: おはよう

<InfoBox type='tip'>
  Struggling with some characters? That's normal! Focus on the tricky ones
  during review. Recognition will come with repetition.
</InfoBox>

## Week 2: Complete Hiragana + Core Vocab 📚

### Day 8: M-Line Hiragana

**Today's goals:**

- Learn ま み む め も

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Quick review (5 min)
2. Learn ま み む め も (15 min)
3. Vocabulary:
   - みみ (mimi - ear)
   - め (me - eye)
   - もも (momo - peach)
4. Practice (15 min)

---

### Day 9: Y-Line & R-Line

**Today's goals:**

- Learn や ゆ よ
- Learn ら り る れ ろ

**Activities (45 min):**

1. Learn や ゆ よ (10 min)
2. Learn ら り る れ ろ (15 min)
3. Vocabulary focus on R-sounds (10 min)
4. Mixed practice (10 min)

**Pronunciation tip:** Japanese "R" is between L and R - tap your tongue!

---

### Day 10: W-Line + N

**Today's goals:**

- Learn わ を ん
- Complete the basic hiragana chart!

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Learn わ を ん (15 min)
2. Understand を as object marker (20 min)
3. CELEBRATE! 🎉 You know all basic hiragana!
4. Full chart review (5 min)

---

### Day 11: Dakuten (Voiced Sounds)

**Today's goals:**

- Learn が ぎ ぐ げ ご, ざ じ ず ぜ ぞ
- Understand the ゛ (dakuten) mark

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Learn concept of voiced sounds (5 min)
2. Practice G and Z rows (20 min)
3. Vocabulary:
   - くすり → ぐすり (medicine)
   - さかな → ざかな... wait, that's wrong! (10 min)
4. Reading practice (5 min)

---

### Day 12: More Dakuten + Handakuten

**Today's goals:**

- Learn だ ぢ づ で ど, ば び ぶ べ ぼ
- Learn ぱ ぴ ぷ ぺ ぽ (handakuten)

**Activities (40 min):**

1. D and B rows (15 min)
2. P row (handakuten ゜) (10 min)
3. Vocabulary with voiced sounds (10 min)
4. Mixed review (5 min)

---

### Day 13: Combination Hiragana

**Today's goals:**

- Learn きゃ きゅ きょ and others
- Understand small ゃ ゅ ょ combinations

**Activities (45 min):**

1. Understand combination concept (5 min)
2. Practice common combinations (25 min):
   - きゃきゅきょ, しゃしゅしょ
   - ちゃちゅちょ, にゃにゅにょ
3. Read words: きょう (today), しゃしん (photo) (10 min)
4. Review (5 min)

---

### Day 14: Week 2 Review 🎯

**Today's goals:**

- Complete hiragana mastery check
- Vocabulary consolidation

**Activities (45 min):**

1. Complete hiragana test (15 min)
2. Vocabulary review: aim for 50 words (15 min)
3. Try reading simple sentences (15 min)

**Week 2 checkpoint:**

- ✅ ALL hiragana (including voiced + combinations)
- ✅ 50+ vocabulary words
- ✅ Can read simple Japanese text!

---

## Week 3: Katakana + Grammar 🔤

### Day 15: Katakana Vowels + First Lines

**Today's goals:**

- Learn katakana ア イ ウ エ オ, カ キ ク ケ コ

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Understand katakana purpose (5 min)
2. Learn vowels + K-line (25 min)
3. Read foreign words: コーヒー (coffee) (10 min)

---

### Day 16: Katakana S-T-N Lines

**Today's goals:**

- Learn サシスセソ, タチツテト, ナニヌネノ

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Quick review (5 min)
2. Learn three new lines (25 min)
3. Read: テスト (test), ノート (notebook) (10 min)

---

### Day 17: Complete Katakana Basics

**Today's goals:**

- Learn remaining basic katakana
- Recognize long vowels (ー)

**Activities (45 min):**

1. Learn ハ-ヲ lines (20 min)
2. Learn long vowel mark ー (10 min)
3. Practice: ラーメン, パーティー (10 min)
4. Review (5 min)

---

### Day 18: First Grammar - です/ます

**Today's goals:**

- Learn です (is/am/are)
- Learn ます (verb ending)
- Form polite sentences

**Activities (45 min):**

1. Learn です patterns (15 min):
   - 私は＿＿です (I am \_\_\_\_)
   - これは＿＿です (This is \_\_\_\_)
2. Learn ます concept (15 min)
3. Practice sentences (15 min)

---

### Day 19: Particles は and を

**Today's goals:**

- Understand topic marker は
- Understand object marker を
- Build simple sentences

**Activities (45 min):**

1. Learn は (topic) function (15 min)
2. Learn を (object) function (15 min)
3. Build sentences:
   - 私はすしを食べます (I eat sushi)
4. Practice (15 min)

<InfoBox type='note'>
  は is written as "ha" but pronounced "wa" when it's the topic particle. This
  is one of Japanese's few exceptions!
</InfoBox>

---

### Day 20: Basic Verbs

**Today's goals:**

- Learn 10 essential verbs
- Practice with particles

**Activities (45 min):**

1. Learn verbs (20 min):
   - 食べます (tabemasu - eat)
   - 飲みます (nomimasu - drink)
   - 行きます (ikimasu - go)
   - 見ます (mimasu - see)
   - します (shimasu - do)
2. Build sentences with each (20 min)
3. Review (5 min)

---

### Day 21: Week 3 Review 🎯

**Activities (45 min):**

1. Katakana review (15 min)
2. Grammar review: make 10 sentences (20 min)
3. Self-assessment (10 min)

**Week 3 checkpoint:**

- ✅ Basic katakana recognition
- ✅ Can use です and ます
- ✅ Understand は and を
- ✅ Know 10 basic verbs

---

## Week 4: Application & Speaking 🗣️

### Day 22: Self-Introduction

**Today's goals:**

- Create your 自己紹介 (self-introduction)
- Practice speaking aloud

**Activities (45 min):**

1. Learn introduction template (15 min)
2. Write YOUR introduction (15 min)
3. Practice speaking 10 times (15 min)

---

### Day 23: Numbers 1-100

**Today's goals:**

- Learn counting 1-100
- Use in real contexts

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Learn 1-10 (10 min)
2. Learn 11-100 pattern (15 min)
3. Practice: phone numbers, prices (15 min)

---

### Day 24: Question Words

**Today's goals:**

- Learn essential question words
- Ask and answer basic questions

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Learn question words (20 min):
   - なに/なん (what)
   - だれ (who)
   - どこ (where)
   - いつ (when)
   - なぜ (why)
   - どう (how)
2. Practice Q&A pairs (20 min)

---

### Day 25: Time & Daily Routine

**Today's goals:**

- Tell time in Japanese
- Describe daily activities

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Learn time expressions (15 min)
2. Vocabulary: 朝 (morning), 昼 (noon), 夜 (night) (10 min)
3. Describe your daily routine (15 min)

---

### Day 26: Listening Practice

**Today's goals:**

- Start training your ear
- Understand natural speed

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Watch beginner Japanese content (20 min)
2. Note words you recognize (10 min)
3. Repeat after audio (10 min)

---

### Day 27: Reading Practice

**Today's goals:**

- Read real Japanese text
- Test comprehension

**Activities (45 min):**

1. Read simple texts/tweets in Japanese (20 min)
2. Identify known words (10 min)
3. Look up new vocabulary (15 min)

---

### Day 28: Writing Practice

**Today's goals:**

- Write simple paragraphs
- Express your thoughts

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Write about yourself (15 min)
2. Write about your hobbies (15 min)
3. Review and correct (10 min)

---

### Day 29: Consolidation Day

**Today's goals:**

- Review weak areas
- Strengthen foundations

**Activities (45 min):**

1. Identify your weak points (5 min)
2. Focus practice on weaknesses (30 min)
3. Overall review (10 min)

---

### Day 30: Celebration & Next Steps! 🎉

**Today's goals:**

- Assess your progress
- Plan Month 2

**Activities (40 min):**

1. Take final assessment:
   - Hiragana (all)
   - Katakana (basics)
   - Vocabulary (100+ words)
   - Grammar (basic sentences)
   - Speaking (self-introduction)
2. Celebrate your achievement! 🎊
3. Plan next month's goals (10 min)

## Tips for Success 💡

### 1. Consistency Over Intensity

30 minutes daily beats 3 hours once a week.

### 2. Use Down Time

Review flashcards on your commute, lunch break, or before bed.

### 3. Speak Out Loud

Don't just read – say words and sentences aloud!

### 4. Don't Skip Days

If you miss a day, don't give up. Just continue tomorrow!

### 5. Celebrate Wins

Each day completed is progress. Be proud!

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if I fall behind?

Don't restart! Pick up where you left off. Progress isn't perfect.

### Is 30 minutes enough?

For beginners, yes! Quality and consistency matter more than quantity.

### What next after 30 days?

Continue with kanji basics, more grammar (past tense, negative), and vocabulary expansion!

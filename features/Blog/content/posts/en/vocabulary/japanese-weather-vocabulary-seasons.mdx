## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I describe today's weather?

Use: 今日は + [weather] + です

- 今日は晴れです - It's sunny today
- 今日は雨です - It's rainy today

### What's the difference between 暑い and 熱い?

Same pronunciation! 暑い = hot weather, 熱い = hot objects.

### How do I say "it's raining"?

雨が降っています (Ame ga futte imasu) = It's raining now.

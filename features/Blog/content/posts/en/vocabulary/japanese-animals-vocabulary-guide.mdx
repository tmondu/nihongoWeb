Ready to meet the animals? どうぶつえんへようこそ! (Welcome to the zoo!) 🐼

  daily conversation. Plus, many Japanese mascots are animals!
</InfoBox>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why are some animals in katakana?

Foreign-origin words or scientific names often use katakana. Native Japanese animals usually have kanji (like 犬, 猫, 熊).

### What's the most popular pet in Japan?

Cats (猫) have overtaken dogs (犬) as the most popular pet in Japan! The cute cat culture is real.

### How do I say "I have a pet..."?

Use: [animal]を飼っています (wo katte imasu)

- 猫を飼っています = I have a cat

Japanese food is one of the most beloved cuisines in the world – and knowing food vocabulary transforms your dining experience! Whether you're ordering at a Tokyo ramen shop, deciphering a sushi menu, or cooking Japanese recipes at home, these words will help you eat (and speak) like a local.

Hungry for some vocabulary? Let's eat! 🍱

<InfoBox type='success'>
  Food vocabulary is the most immediately useful Japanese you can learn! You'll
  use these words at every meal during any Japan trip.
</InfoBox>

### Ordering Sushi

> サーモン二貫ください
> Saamon ni-kan kudasai
> Two pieces of salmon, please

> Without wasabi, please

### Ordering Ramen

> 醤油ラーメン、麺固めで
> Shouyu raamen, men katame de
> Soy sauce ramen with firm noodles

> 味玉追加でお願いします
> Ajitama tsuika de onegai shimasu
> Add an egg, please

<InfoBox type='note'>
  Many ramen shops use vending machines (食券機 - shokkenki) to order. Buy a
  ticket for your desired ramen, then hand it to staff!
</InfoBox>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I order at a Japanese restaurant?

1. Look at the menu or plastic food displays
2. Say [item] をください / おねがいします
3. For quantity: [item] を [number] つ ください

### What if I can't read the menu?

- Point and say これをください (This one, please)
- Ask おすすめは？ (Recommendations?)
- Look for picture menus

### Is it okay to slurp noodles?

YES! Slurping ramen and soba is expected and shows you're enjoying the food. It also helps cool the noodles and enhances flavor!

### Do I tip at restaurants?

NO! Tipping is not customary in Japan and can even be confusing or offensive. Great service is simply expected.
